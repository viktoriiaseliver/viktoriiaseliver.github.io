Frequently asked

The countdown doesn't match the time in my calendar

That is the point. The app counts down not to the start of the event but to the moment you need to leave: start minus travel minus getting ready. The line under the big number always spells out the leave time and the buffer.

Travel time isn't calculated

Three things are needed: an address on the event, location turned on (Settings → Journey) and a network connection. If something is missing the app says so on the journey card and offers "Enter manually" — a number you type counts exactly like a calculated one and never goes stale.

The widget looks frozen or out of date

Widgets read what the app writes for them. Open the app and the widget catches up. iOS decides how often widgets are woken, and wakes them less often in Low Power Mode.

No notifications

Check three things: notification permission in system settings; the "Remind before leaving" switch under Lateness; and whether "Only for events with an address or attendees" is filtering out this particular event.

The Live Activity doesn't appear

It starts when you open the app and there is an hour or less until you need to leave. iOS lets nobody start one from the background on a schedule without server push notifications — and this app has no servers by design. The "time to leave" notification arrives regardless.

The voice stays silent

The voice speaks while the app is open, and when you open it from a notification. iOS does not allow speech from the background. Use "Test" in Appearance & voice to hear it.

I changed the language and the interface didn't change

Dates and numbers change immediately; the rest of the text changes when the app is next launched. The language screen says so.

I bought Pro but the themes are still locked

Tap "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen or in About. If Pro was bought on another device, use the same Apple ID. Managing or cancelling the subscription happens in the App Store; there is a link in About.

How do I delete everything the app knows about me

Delete the app: settings, hidden events and voice recordings go with it. Your calendar is untouched — the app only reads it and creates events when you ask. Details in the privacy policy.

The app lost calendar access

System Settings → Privacy → Calendars. Full access is required: with write-only access there is nothing to show, and the app says so.
