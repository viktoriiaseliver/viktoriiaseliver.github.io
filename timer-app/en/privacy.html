Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026. Updated: Together section added.

English · Русский · Українська

In short

The app collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Everything it works out is worked out on your phone and never leaves it.

The developer has no access to your calendar, your location, your voice recordings or your purchases.

Who is responsible

Five More Minutes is made by Viktoriia Seliverstova, an independent developer. You can get in touch on the support page.

What the app uses, and why

"Uses" here means "reads on your device in order to show you a countdown". None of the categories below is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Calendar events: title, time, location, attendees, meeting link To work out when you need to leave and show it on screen, in widgets and on the Lock Screen In memory and in the app's protected container on your device. Only the short upcoming list is written there, to be read by the app's own widgets
Location, while the app is open To estimate travel time to an event's address using Apple Maps Never stored. The calculation happens on the device and stays there
Your settings: getting-ready buffer, travel mode, warning texts, hidden events, theme, tone, language So the app behaves the way you decided On the device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which the developer cannot access
Voice recordings (the "own phrases" feature, only if you turn it on) To say in your own words that it is time to leave Only in the app's container on the device. Never uploaded, not even to iCloud
Pro purchase To unlock themes, tone and voice Verified with Apple through StoreKit. The app only stores a "purchased" flag on the device

What the app does not do

  • no accounts, no sign-up;
  • no data sent to the developer's servers — there are none;
  • no analytics, ad networks, trackers or third-party libraries;
  • no tracking across apps or websites (App Tracking Transparency does not apply — there is no tracking);
  • no selling or sharing of data;
  • no reading of your meetings for any purpose other than showing the countdown.

Where data can leave the device

Because you tapped — two cases:

  • "I'll be late" opens the system SMS or mail composer with prepared text. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and it is sent by your own messaging account, not by the app.
  • "Directions" opens your chosen maps app and passes it the event address.

Without you doing anything — one case:

  • Travel estimates query Apple's maps service to build a route. This happens on its own, when the app reads your calendar: your departure time is worked out ahead of time, because otherwise you would learn it late — at the moment it had already arrived. It is done for the few nearest events that have an address. The request follows Apple's own privacy terms; the developer does not see it.

Together: the free time you choose to share

Together is a separate feature, off by default. Until you turn it on with explicit consent, nothing leaves your device and everything above holds without qualification.

If you do turn it on, exactly three things leave the device:

  • the time ranges you marked yourself — not all of your free time, only what you marked;
  • the range's label from a fixed list ("a walk", "coffee") — there is no free-text field;
  • the name you chose for yourself.

Nothing else goes: no event titles, no locations, no calendar, no indication of how much you have on, no location data.

Where it lives: in your own iCloud, in a private CloudKit database. Not on a developer's server — there is none. Access is granted through Apple's own mechanism (CKShare) only to the people you sent an invitation link to. Nobody can look you up by email or phone: that capability does not exist, Apple removed the relevant interface in iOS 17.

Your identity is your Apple ID. The app creates no accounts of its own, stores no passwords and never asks for access to your contacts.

How to delete it: Settings → Friends → My data. The same screen shows exactly what is stored. "Remove all my windows" erases the ranges and keeps your circle; "Turn Together off" deletes the ranges, the circle, your name and the consent itself. There is no deactivate-without-delete.

Your name and reports. Your name is checked before publication, because other people see it. Anyone in your circle can be removed, blocked (which works both ways) and reported — a report arrives by email at the address on the support page.

iCloud

Settings sync between your devices through your iCloud (Key-Value Storage). That is your private Apple storage; the developer does not and cannot look into it. You can stop it by turning iCloud off for the app in system settings. Travel estimates are deliberately not synced — they are measured from one particular device's location.

Children

The app is not directed at children, collects no data and shows no ads. Age rating 4+.

Your rights

Because the developer never receives or stores your data, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from the developer. Everything the app keeps lives on your device and goes away with it: delete the app and your settings, hidden events and voice recordings are gone. Calendar, location, microphone and notification permissions can be withdrawn at any time in system settings; the app keeps working and explains what became unavailable.

If you are in the EU or the UK: the legal basis for on-device processing is performance of our agreement with you (showing the countdown) together with your consent expressed through system permissions. No transfer off the device takes place that would require a separate basis.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Material changes will be described in the App Store release notes.
