Five More Minutes

Not "at 4:30 pm". "In 47 minutes".

English · Русский

The app does not show you when an event starts. It shows how long you have before you need to leave: start time minus travel minus getting ready. The countdown lives on the home screen, in widgets, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Everything is worked out on the device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads.
