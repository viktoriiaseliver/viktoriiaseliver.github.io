Frequently asked

The receipt says "does not add up"

The lines and the total differ by more than two cents. Usually one digit was lost while reading: "1.5" where the paper says "1.54". Tap the line and fix the number. The app deliberately does not stretch the tolerance to hide this — a quietly wrong number is worse than a visible question.

Why do I only confirm some lines, not the whole receipt

Only the lines the app is unsure about are sent for confirmation. Confirming a whole receipt line by line teaches you to tap through without reading, and then the app learns your inattention along with the words.

The forecast is not shown

It appears only when there is enough to base it on: enough receipts, enough days between the first and the last, and enough of your card spending covered. When one of those is missing the app says which one, instead of showing a number it cannot support.

My receipt has no date

Many shops print the date at the very bottom, and a screenshot of the top of the receipt cuts it off. Those receipts are listed separately on the month screen with a "no date" marker — they are not lost and not silently dropped into the wrong month. Set the date there in one tap.

I added the same receipt twice

The second one is not saved. A duplicate would distort both the month and the price history, so the app keeps the first and tells you the second was already there. Nothing is wrong on your side.

The Prices tab is empty

A price needs something to be compared with. The tab fills up when the same product appears in two different receipts — until then there is one price and nothing to say about it. Two lines of the same receipt are not a history.

Why does it say a product got more expensive when I did not notice

The app compares the latest price you paid with the cheapest one it has seen for the same product in the same unit, and only mentions it when the difference is large enough to be worth your attention. Different units are never compared: a price per kilo and a price per item are different things.

How do I delete everything the app knows about me

Delete the app. The receipts, the dictionary it learned and your settings go with it. There is no copy anywhere else — see the privacy policy.

Will my receipts appear on my other iPhone

Not yet. Today they live on the device you added them to. Sync through your own private iCloud is planned; until it ships, the privacy policy describes exactly what happens, and it will be updated the day that changes.
