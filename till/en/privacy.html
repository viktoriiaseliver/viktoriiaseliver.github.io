Till

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026.

English · Français

In short

Till collects no data about you. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your receipts are read on your iPhone, kept on your iPhone, and never sent anywhere.

The developer never receives your receipts, your prices, your photos or your purchases — and has no way to.

Who is responsible

Till is made by Viktoriia Seliverstova, an independent developer. You can get in touch on the support page.

What the app uses, and why

"Uses" here means "reads on your device in order to show you your own spending". None of the categories below is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Receipts you add: shop, date, total, the lines and their prices To show what you spent this month, and what the same product costs in different shops In the app's own database on your iPhone
The photo, screenshot or PDF of a receipt To read the text off it Read on the device using Apple's own text recognition. The image is not uploaded anywhere
Your corrections when you confirm a line So the app recognises the same product next time without asking In the app's dictionary on your iPhone
Pro purchase To unlock the paid features Verified with Apple through StoreKit. The app only stores a "purchased" flag on the device

What the app does not do

  • no account, no sign-up, no loyalty card;
  • no servers — the developer does not run any, and the app makes no network requests of its own;
  • no analytics, ad networks, trackers or third-party libraries;
  • no tracking across apps or websites (App Tracking Transparency does not apply — there is no tracking);
  • no selling or sharing of data;
  • no reading of your photo library beyond the images you pick yourself.

Where data can leave the device

Nowhere, today. This is not a summary of the section above — it is the whole section. Till has no code that sends anything off the device, and no address it could send anything to.

Two things are worth naming precisely, because they sound like exceptions and are not:

  • Reading a receipt uses Apple's text recognition, which runs on your iPhone. The picture is not uploaded to Apple or to anyone else.
  • Buying Pro goes through the App Store, so Apple knows you bought it, the same way Apple knows about every purchase. The developer sees only anonymous sales totals, never who you are.

Sync between devices

There is none yet, and this page will say so until there is. Your receipts live on the iPhone you added them to. They are not copied to another device, not to a server, and not to iCloud.

Syncing through your own private iCloud is planned. When it ships, this page will be rewritten with a new date, and the release notes in the App Store will say so. Until that happens, treat this paragraph as the truth and anything else you may have read as out of date.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone — to iCloud or to a computer — the backup contains the app's data, because it contains everything on the phone. That backup is yours and is governed by Apple's terms, not by this app. The developer has no access to it.

Children

The app is not directed at children, collects no data and shows no ads. Age rating 4+.

Your rights

Because the developer never receives or stores your data, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from the developer. Everything the app keeps lives on your device and goes away with it: delete the app and your receipts, your dictionary and your settings are gone with it.

If you are in the EU or the UK: the legal basis for on-device processing is performance of our agreement with you (showing you your own spending) together with your consent expressed through system permissions. No transfer off the device takes place that would require a separate basis.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Material changes will be described in the App Store release notes.
