Till

Your real prices, shop by shop.

English · Français

Till reads your grocery receipts — a PDF from an email, a screenshot, or a photo — and shows what you actually spent this month and what the same product costs in different shops.

Everything is read and kept on your iPhone. No account, no loyalty card, no server.
