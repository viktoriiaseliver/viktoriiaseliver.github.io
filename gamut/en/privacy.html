In short

Gamut is a feed-planning app. Everything it does, it does on your device. This page says the same thing as the app's "Terms & Privacy" screen. Every claim below is checked by the app's own automated tests.

Your data stays on your phone

Your feed, palettes and photos live in the app's folder on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Gamut has no server.

No account

No sign-up, no login through other services. The app does not know who you are.

Colour is computed on the device

Photo analysis uses the system's own frameworks. Your pictures never leave the phone.

No analytics, no tracking

No third-party libraries, no counters, no advertising identifiers. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

The action journal stays on the phone too

The app writes down what it does — screens opened, tiles edited, a free-tier limit hit — into a plain text file in its own folder. It exists so that "the app got in my way here" can be looked at later. It holds no names, no photos, no text you typed: only what happened and when. The file never leaves your phone. Read it, share it yourself, erase it or switch it off in Settings → Journal.

Photos access

Gamut picks photos through the system photo picker. That needs no permission and shows the app only the pictures you choose. The one permission the app asks for is add-only access to Photos, to save the preview you export. It cannot read your library.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. There is no push server.

How to take your data with you

The Gamut folder is visible in the Files app. Copy it to iCloud Drive before deleting the app, and put it back afterwards.

Purchases

Gamut Pro is sold through the App Store. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. The app never sees your card details.

Changes

If any of this changes — say a server component ever appears — this page and the in-app screen change together, and the App Store privacy label is updated.
