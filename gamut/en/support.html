Common questions

The import picker shows no screenshots

Any screenshot of a three-column profile grid works. It does not have to be tagged as a screenshot by the system — pick it from "All Photos".

I bought Pro but the app says I'm on the free plan

Open More → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. If Pro was bought under another one, sign in with it.

I want my data out

The Gamut folder is visible in the Files app. Copy it to iCloud Drive — everything the app knows is in there.

Something got in my way and I want to show you what happened

The app keeps a journal of what it does: a plain text file on your phone, with no names, no photos and no typed text. Open Settings → Journal → Share the journal and send it with your message. It turns "it did something odd here" into something checkable. If you would rather it kept nothing, switch the journal off on the same screen.

Something looks broken in another language

Every string is translated into English, French and Ukrainian, and the translations are checked by tests. If you see the wrong language, tell us which screen.

How do I delete everything the app knows about me

Delete the app. The feed, the palettes, the imported pictures and the journal all live in its own folder and go away with it. Nothing is stored anywhere else — see the privacy policy.
