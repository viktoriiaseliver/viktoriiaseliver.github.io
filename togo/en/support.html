Common questions

Why does the verdict say “not sure”?

It needs three or four days of records to learn your dog’s intervals. Until then it admits it does not know rather than inventing an answer. The “Taking him out” button stays first in every case: the app does not know better than you.

Where is the Health tab?

It arrives with a course of treatment and leaves with it: you switch it on in the dog’s profile and off with “Course finished” at the bottom of Health. The records stay in the history and in the vet report.

Two people logged the same thing

If two people log the same thing within 20 minutes, the app shows both of them a merge card. It never silently overwrites someone else’s entry.

How do I get my records out?

Settings → Data and privacy — export to CSV and delete everything, with no emails and no waiting. The CSV opens in Numbers and Excel.

This app is not a medical device

Togo does not diagnose and does not prescribe doses. Decisions about your dog’s health are made by you and your vet; the app shows what you put into it yourself.
